TaskCash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

TaskCash ("the app") lets you earn reward coins by completing simple in-app activities and request payouts. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, Supabase).
  • Device identifier: an Android device identifier, used only to allow one account per phone and prevent fraud.
  • Activity data: your coin balance, rewards earned, quiz answers, referral code and referrals, and withdrawal requests.
  • Payout details: the mobile wallet number, UPI ID or PayPal email you enter when requesting a withdrawal.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and device information to show ads (see Google's privacy policy).

How we use information

  • To run the app, credit your rewards and process withdrawals.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To show ads, which fund the app.
  • To contact you about your account or payouts.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with service providers that run the app: Supabase (database and login) and Google AdMob (advertising). Payout details are used only to send your payment.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Reward amounts and balances are controlled on our servers.

Your choices and account deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app: Wallet > Delete my account. You can also request deletion by emailing us at the address below. Withdrawal records may be kept in anonymous form for accounting.

Children

TaskCash is not intended for children under 13 (or under 18 where payouts require adulthood). We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always available at this page.
